# Bakery-Sales-Analysis-Performance
This is an exploratory sales data analysis project using Excel to discover sales trend over the years and product performance.
# Project Overview
This project analyzes bakery sales data to discover business performance and identify sales patterns, and trends over the years(2021 -2022).
This analysis was conducted using Excel, with interactive visualizations created to generate meaningful insights for better business decision making.
# Objectives
The objectives of the products are to:
Analyze overall sales perfprmance.
Get the total quantity of article sold.
Examine sales trend between  2021 and 2022.
Analyze quantity trend acrosss the two years.
Identify the top 10 article by sales. 
Identify the top 10 article by quantity.
# Dataset
The dataset contains bakery sales transaction record used to analyze sales and quantity performance.
The raw dataset was cleaned and prepared for analysis before creating the dashboard.
# Data cleaning and preparation
The following steps were carried on the dataset:
Removed unnecessary column.
Changed datatypes.
Filtered rows.
Changed negative row values to absolute values.
Created calculated column.
Prepared the data for analysis using Excel.

## Quantity Trend Analysis

## Top 10 article by quantity

# Key Insights
Sales increased and decreased drastically in some certain months across the years.
Some products recorded higher quantity sales and lowers sales across the years.
Traditional Baquette generated the highest revenue followed by Formule Sandwich .
Tradiitonal Baquette has the highest quantity sold followed by Croissant.
